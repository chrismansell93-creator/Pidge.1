## Full description
Pidge shows people around you in a tight photo grid, sorted by distance.

See who’s nearby, open a profile, send a tap, and chat. Filter by online, fresh, gender, tribe, and range. Pull down to refresh your location.

Limited is free: the 50 closest people, adverts, and 8 taps a day.
Unlimited is £10 a month through Google Play: the full grid, no ads, unlimited taps.

You must be 18 or older. Meet in public. Report abuse in the app. Cancel Unlimited in Google Play.
